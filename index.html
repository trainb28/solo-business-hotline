Why this doesn't exist yet

You've already tried the other three options.

Nearly 30 million Americans now run a business with no employees. That group has grown more than twice as fast as businesses that hire, every year for over a decade. The support for them has not kept up, and most of what exists is one dimensional.

Hire a consulting firm
Priced for somebody else

Fifteen to thirty grand for a project, and that's the friendly end. Those firms are built for companies with a budget line and a procurement process. You have neither, and you shouldn't need either.

Read the startup advice
Wrong business entirely

Every founder playbook assumes cofounders, funding, a team and a plan to scale to a hundred people. You're not building a rocket. You're building a very good living for one person, and almost none of that advice survives the translation.
