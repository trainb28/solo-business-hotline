Questions about the questions

Fair enough.

What does "unlimited" actually mean?

Ask as often as you want. No cap, no credits, no hourly billing. Five questions this week is fine. So is none for three months.

How fast do I get an answer?

Usually same day, almost always within 24 hours. Other members often get there before I do, which is frequently better anyway.

How many consultations do I get?

Book one when you genuinely need one. Most members use a couple a year. If somebody starts booking weekly we'll have a friendly conversation about it.

I'm just getting started. Do I belong here?

Yes. Members range from first client to well past six figures, and some still have a job while they build. Nobody here is going to make you feel behind.

What if I barely use it?

Then it was a couple bucks a day to know an expert was there, which is the point. Plenty of members go quiet for months and then use it hard for two weeks when something breaks. That's the design, not a failure to get your money's worth.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your own account, no email required and no exit interview. I'd rather you leave easily than stay annoyed.
